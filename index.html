Small studies, lasting growth

Six ways He led

Choose an attribute. Read the scripture, then consider the question in your own life.

01 / Humility

He served beside others.

At the Last Supper, Jesus washed His disciples’ feet and invited them to follow His example.

John 13:1–17 ↗
Consider: What need can I meet quietly this week?
02 / Love

He saw the individual.

The Savior made time to welcome and bless children when others tried to send them away.

Mark 10:13–16 ↗
Consider: Who might need to feel seen and included?
03 / Compassion

He noticed a need.

Jesus looked on the multitude with compassion, taught them, and helped provide food.

Mark 6:34–44 ↗
Consider: What do the people I serve need most?
04 / Invitation

He invited people to come.

Rather than simply telling two disciples where He stayed, Jesus invited them to “come and see.”

John 1:35–39 ↗
Consider: How can I invite instead of pressure?
05 / Listening

He listened with care.

On the road, the Savior gave two disciples space to share what was in their hearts.

Luke 24:13–19 ↗
Consider: Who could I listen to more fully?
06 / Prayer

He sought the Father.

Before choosing His Apostles, Jesus spent the night in prayer to God.

Luke 6:12–13 ↗
Consider: Where do I need heaven’s direction?
